## Brackeys' VFX Bundle (flipbooks)

- Source: https://brackeysgames.itch.io/brackeys-vfx-bundle
- Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0). The licence and credits file is at `Assets/Art/External/Brackeys VFX Bundle/LICENSE & CREDITS.txt`.
- The flipbooks in the bundle are by Thomas Iché. Only the 14 flipbook sheets are included, converted from TGA to PNG.
- Modification: each frame's alpha has a soft radial fade baked in near the edge of its cell, because several frames reach the cell border and would otherwise show as hard-edged squares. The fire sheets have no alpha channel, so their alpha is taken from brightness before the same fade.

| Flipbook | Used for |
| :--- | :--- |

| explosion_smoke_01_8x8 | Sentai coloured blast smoke |
| flame_01_16x4 | Sentai flame plumes, boss flames behind the throne |

| fire_01_8x8 | Fire punches, spinning-kick arc, palm blast |
